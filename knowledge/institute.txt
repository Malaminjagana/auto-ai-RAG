Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute

Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute is an educational institution
in The Gambia.

The institute provides Arabic and Islamic education.

Academic Programs:

1. Arabic & Islamic Studies
The main program focuses on Arabic language and Islamic education.

2. Qur'an Memorization (Hifz)
Students can study and memorize the Qur'an.

3. English Language Program
The institute provides English language education.

The institute also provides private English programs for students.
